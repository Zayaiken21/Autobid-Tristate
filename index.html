Making a smart call

Know which auctions you can actually use

Copart and IAA sell many lots to the public, but some salvage/parts-only lots in NY and NJ require a dealer or dismantler license — use a licensed broker for those. Manheim and ACV are dealer-only. NYC Sheriff/City Marshal sales are open to the public, in person, cash only.

NYC Marshal/Sheriff sales

You buy the debtor's "right, title and interest" with no warranty. Keys are often missing, the car may need a tow, and it must be removed by 5 PM on sale day or storage charges start. Run every VIN in the Analyze tab before you go.

Red flags that should lower your bid

  • Flood/water damage — especially common in the NY/NJ coastal area after storms
  • Fire, rollover, or deployed airbags (airbag kits are expensive)
  • "Not actual" odometer, no keys, does not start
  • Open safety recalls (free to fix at a dealer, but confirm parts availability)

Rules of thumb

  • Set your max bid before the sale and don't chase it
  • Pad repair estimates 20–30% for hidden damage
  • Get a full history report (Carfax/AutoCheck) on anything you're serious about
  • Salvage cars in NY need a salvage examination; NJ needs an MVC salvage inspection before re-titling
